Working prototype

Rock vs mineral image classifier

A model that looks at a photo and says whether it shows a rock or a mineral. I trained it in a notebook, shrank it, and ran it on a Raspberry Pi with a local web interface.

Data
580 photos from Kaggle. Rocks: granite, sandstone. Minerals: biotite, chrysocolla, malachite, pyrite.
Model
MobileNetV2 with transfer learning, trained for 10 epochs.
Result
About 99% on training photos, 75–80% on unseen photos. The gap shows overfitting, which I’m working to fix.
Runs on
Raspberry Pi, as a 2.6 MB TensorFlow Lite model.
