Work I’ve completed

Working prototype

Rock vs mineral image classifier

Problem. Telling rocks and minerals apart from a photo, on a device small enough to carry into the field.

  • Trained on 580 photos: granite and sandstone as rocks; biotite, chrysocolla, malachite and pyrite as minerals.
  • MobileNetV2 transfer learning, then converted to a 2.6 MB quantized TensorFlow Lite model.
  • Runs on a Raspberry Pi with a local web interface.

My part. Embedded AI lab project: I worked through training, evaluation and deployment on the Pi.

Result. About 99% accuracy on training photos and 75–80% on unseen photos. The gap is overfitting, which I plan to reduce with more varied data and by freezing the base model.
